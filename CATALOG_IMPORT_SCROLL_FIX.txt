Daily Rebuild — Data & Backup Scroll Fix
Date: August 6, 2026

Problem
- The Data & Backup feature was hosted inside the fixed-height RebuildFeaturePage content area.
- BackupRestoreFeature used a non-scrollable Column with fillMaxWidth().
- Content below the visible height, including Food Catalog Import, was clipped and unreachable on smaller screens.

Fix
- BackupRestoreFeature now fills the available content area and applies verticalScroll(rememberScrollState()) to its root Column.
- Backup, Food Catalog Import, backup coverage, and emergency restore sections now participate in one continuous scroll container.
- Dialogs retain their own independent scrolling.

Test
1. Open More.
2. Open Data & Backup.
3. Swipe upward on the page.
4. Confirm Food Catalog Import is reachable and Select Catalog JSON can be tapped.
5. Continue scrolling and confirm backup coverage and Emergency Restore Point remain reachable when present.
